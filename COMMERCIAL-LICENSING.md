# AgentVN Commercial Licensing

AgentVN is an independently developed software project, not a company.
AquaLite is the individual developer, public copyright identity, Licensor,
and party authorized to grant commercial licenses.

Public repository documents may identify the Licensor only as AquaLite. A
commercial agreement should be signed using the Licensor's legal name followed
by `publicly known as AquaLite`; the legal name does not need to be published
in this repository.

The public AgentVN license permits source inspection, study, private
modification, contributions, and use by individuals or studios to make and
sell games and other works created with AgentVN. It does **not** permit
commercial exploitation of the AgentVN editor or authoring system itself.

You do not need a separate AgentVN commercial license to use the editor
internally to make or sell a visual novel, game, image, audio work, text,
video, or data result. The conditions for exported runtime components still
apply.

You should request a separate commercial license if you want to:

- sell or commercially redistribute AgentVN or a modified version;
- bundle AgentVN as a commercial authoring product;
- integrate AgentVN source into proprietary commercial software;
- offer AgentVN functionality through SaaS, API, hosting, or a managed
  service;
- build a competing commercial editor, player platform, generation service,
  or automation service from AgentVN; or
- obtain support, warranties, indemnities, private features, or negotiated
  terms.

## Contact

Until a dedicated licensing email is published, open a GitHub Discussion in
the official AgentVN repository with the title `Commercial License Request`.
Do not include confidential information in the public post. The maintainer
will provide a private contact channel.

Before publishing the repository, the owner should replace this temporary
contact process with a monitored address such as `licensing@example.com`.

Submitting a request does not grant permission. Commercial rights exist only
after the individual publicly known as AquaLite and the applicant sign a
separate written agreement using legally verifiable identities.
