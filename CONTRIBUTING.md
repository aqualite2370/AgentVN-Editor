# Contributing to AgentVN

Issues, focused patches, and pull requests are welcome. Documentation,
reproduction cases, tests, accessibility improvements, and narrowly scoped
fixes are especially useful.

## Before contributing

1. Do not include API keys, private prompts, personal data, proprietary
   novels, unlicensed media, generated assets without provenance, or build
   artifacts.
2. State whether code or assets are original and identify any third-party
   source and license.
3. Keep changes focused and include tests or a reproducible verification
   method where practical.
4. Retain existing copyright, license, trademark, and attribution notices.

## Issues, patches, and pull requests

Opening an issue does not transfer ownership of its text or attachments.
Small diagnostic patches may be reviewed before a Contributor License
Agreement is available, but the project should not merge substantial external
code until an appropriate CLA process is active.

Because AgentVN uses a source-available public license and may also issue
separate commercial licenses, a Developer Certificate of Origin alone is not
enough: it confirms provenance but does not give the maintainer the clear
relicensing rights needed for dual licensing.

The recommended CLA should:

- let contributors retain copyright;
- grant the project owner a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free
  copyright license, including rights to use, reproduce, modify, distribute,
  sublicense, and relicense the contribution;
- include a patent license for claims necessarily infringed by the
  contribution, with a conventional patent-litigation termination clause;
- confirm that the contributor is entitled to submit the work and has
  disclosed third-party material;
- permit the contribution to be offered under both the public
  source-available license and separate commercial licenses; and
- provide both individual and corporate forms where an employer may own the
  work.

The project owner and public copyright identity is AquaLite. Until a
project-specific CLA naming AquaLite is published, maintainers should accept
Issues and discussion, but either defer external PR merges or obtain a signed
written agreement for each external contribution.

The public CLA may refer to AquaLite as the project owner, but the signature
and private agreement records should identify the owner's legal name as
`publicly known as AquaLite`. The legal name does not need to appear in the
public repository.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that public discussion and review may
take place. Submission alone does not guarantee acceptance.
